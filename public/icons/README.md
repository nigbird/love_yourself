To complete your PWA setup, please add your app icon files in this directory.

Based on the `manifest.json` file, you should provide at least two sizes:

1.  A 192x192 pixel icon named `icon-192x192.png`
2.  A 512x512 pixel icon named `icon-512x512.png`

You can also add an `apple-touch-icon.png` (sized 180x180) here for better support on iOS devices.
